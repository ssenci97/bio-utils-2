synced_structure

Synchronized Biopython structures: chain mapping, contacts, contact maps, interfaces, 3D views, and HADDOCK3 docking.

A Biopython structure wrapper that keeps every observed residue linked to its position in the reference sequence, and builds contacts, contact maps, interface metrics, HADDOCK3 dockings, tables and 3D views on top of that link. Installation and a quick start are in the README.

Numbers and units

  • Indices are 1-based in every returned table.
  • nm for contacts: contact_table, contact matrices, contact maps and every contact cutoff=.
  • Å and Å² for interface metrics and docking; names say _a or _A where it matters.
  • A chain can be named by its key, label ID or author ID; keys are matched first.

Vocabulary

Concepts

A deposited structure is an incomplete copy of a biological sequence: some residues are not modelled, numbering is chosen by the depositors, and every chain has two IDs. Every table reports all of these names.

125pdb_resnumauthor number in the file
1sequence_indexposition in the observed sequence
6reference_indexposition in the reference sequence

The first modelled residue of chain A of 5D39 has three numbers. Only reference_index means the same residue in every chain, structure and model.

TermMeaning

label ID chain_idChain ID assigned by the PDB (label_asym_id).
author ID auth_chain_idChain ID chosen by the depositors (auth_asym_id); the one most viewers show. The same letter can name a different chain in the other system.

reference sequenceFull sequence of the chain's entity, from the mmCIF file or set by you.

moietyType of a residue: protein, nucleic or other (ligands, ions, water). Polymer residues are protein and nucleic.
contact pointOne point per residue for the ca scheme: Cα (protein), C4′ (nucleic), heavy-atom centroid (other).
contact schemeca: distance between contact points. sidechain: closest side-chain (protein) or base (nucleic) heavy atoms.
contact typeinter (between chains), intra (within a chain) or all.

interfaceResidues of two chains that bury surface against each other, measured with heavy atoms.
